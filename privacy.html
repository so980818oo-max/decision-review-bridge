Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Scope

This opt-in service processes the connected account owner's Riot identity and official VALORANT match data to provide post-match coaching.

Data use

Identity and match data are requested only after authorization through Riot Sign On. They are used to show the connected player their own completed-match review. The service does not sell data, create public player profiles, enable opponent scouting, or use match data for advertising.

Storage and security

The current prototype uses sample data and stores no Riot player data. In production, API and RSO credentials remain server-side, transport uses HTTPS, and only the minimum data needed for a review is processed.

Player control

A player may disconnect the service or revoke Riot authorization. Reviews are not made public by default. Account and deletion controls will be available before live launch.

Contact

Contact details will be added before the public beta. Until then, questions can be submitted through the product's Riot Developer Portal application.
