COACHING METHOD

Three decisions.
One habit to test next.

The service deliberately avoids overwhelming score dumps. Each review selects a few high-impact moments, explains the available information, compares the choice made with a realistic alternative, and ends with one question for the player.

  • Official Riot APIs only
  • Completed matches only
  • Account-owner opt-in through RSO
  • Read-only processing; no gameplay automation
